The first version you can try:

- **Calls to action**: waitlist, early access with qualifying questions, demo booking, pre-order, contact form — one line in `site.config.ts` switches between them.
- **Leads**: your own Cloudflare Worker and D1 database with double opt-in, a real queue with referrals and an honest counter — or one of eight services, from Web3Forms to Mailchimp.
- **Fake-door pricing** that always discloses the test and never takes money.
- **Legal pack**: privacy, cookies, terms, pre-orders and imprint as starting-point drafts, plus a self-service data request page.
