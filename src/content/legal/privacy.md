## What we collect

**When you sign up or send a form**, we collect what you type: your email address and, depending on the form, your name, a message and your answers to a few questions about your needs. We also store the wording of the consent text you saw, the page you signed up on, the address of the page that sent you here, and any campaign tags in the link you followed (such as `utm_source`) or the referral code of the person who invited you.

**To keep the forms free of abuse**, we store a one-way hash of your IP address (never the address itself) for {{IP_RETENTION_DAYS}} days, then delete it.

**When you choose a plan on the pricing page**, we record which plan you chose, the day and the same IP hash, so one person is counted once. While the pricing page tests interest, choosing a plan never takes payment.

**Visit statistics**, if this site uses them, are listed under "Who handles your data". They do not identify you.

## Why we use it and on what basis

- **To send you what you signed up for** — the launch news, a reply to your message, your place in the queue. Basis: your consent, given when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email or on the [data request page](/legal/data-request/).
- **To confirm your address** — we send one email with a link before you count as signed up.
- **To understand which ideas, plans and channels people are interested in** — counting signups and plan choices, by source. Basis: our legitimate interest in deciding what to build, using as little data as possible.
- **To stop spam and abuse** — the IP hash and, where enabled, a bot check. Basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the forms working.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.
