## Statistics without cookies

If this site counts visits, it uses a cookieless service (listed in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/)) that does not store anything in your browser. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the site does not load statistics at all.

If the site ever uses statistics that need cookies, it asks first with a banner, loads them only after you accept, and lets you change your mind from "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page.

## Booking and checkout pages

Buttons that open a booking calendar or a checkout take you to another service's page. That service may use its own cookies there; its own policy applies.

## How to control cookies

Your browser lets you see and delete cookies and site data, and block them. Blocking them does not stop this site from working.
